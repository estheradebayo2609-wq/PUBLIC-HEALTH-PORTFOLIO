Project 2: Girls in Charge Foundation (GIC)

Role: Public Health Program Intern

Project: Empower to Succeed Conference 3.0

Project Overview

I supported the planning and implementation of the Empower to Succeed Conference 3.0, an educational development program by the Girls in Charge Foundation designed to support secondary school students preparing for major examinations, including WAEC, NECO, and JAMB. Professionals and representatives from the respective examination bodies were invited to provide students with relevant guidance, practical information, and strategies for navigating their examinations successfully.

My Contributions

* Supported the planning and implementation of the conference, contributing to the coordination of program activities.
* Collaborated with the Communications Department to create campaign videos and support publicity efforts for the conference.
* Served as Head of the Registration Team, coordinating the registration process and ensuring an organized participant check-in experience.
* Facilitated the program, engaging participants and supporting the smooth delivery of the conference.

Impact & Outcome

The conference recorded 861 registered students from 38 schools, providing secondary school students with access to relevant information, guidance, and direct engagement with professionals from examination bodies.

Skills Demonstrated

Program Planning & Implementation • Event Coordination • Team Leadership • Communication & Publicity • Facilitation • Stakeholder Engagement • Registration & Participant Management

Lessons Learned

Interning at GIC and supporting the Programs Department taught me the importance of teamwork, communication, and effective planning in program delivery.

Serving as the Registration Team Lead and facilitating the program strengthened my leadership, coordination, communication, and public speaking skills.
