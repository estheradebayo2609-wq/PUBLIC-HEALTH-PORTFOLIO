Project 1: Centre for Women’s Health and Information (CEWHIN)i

Role: Public Health Program Intern

Project: Webinar on Intimate Partner Violence

Project Overview

During my internship at the Centre for Women’s Health and Information (CEWHIN), I organized and coordinated my first webinar focused on Intimate Partner Violence (IPV) and its impact on the mental health of undergraduates in Nigeria.

The program aimed to raise awareness about intimate partner violence among young people, highlight its potential mental health consequences, and encourage informed conversations around prevention, support, and healthy relationships.

My Contribution

* Conceptualized and organized the webinar as part of the program activities.
* Supported planning, coordination, and event logistics.
* Assisted with publicity and participant engagement.
* Contributed to documentation, monitoring, and evaluation to track the project’s activities.

Impact & Outcome

The webinar reached 58 undergraduate students in Nigeria. To assess the impact of the program, participants completed a pre-event assessment before the webinar and a post-event assessment after the webinar. A comparison of the responses indicated an increase in participants’ level of awareness and understanding of intimate partner violence and its impact on mental health following the webinar.

Lessons Learned

Organizing the webinar gave me practical experience in program coordination, communication, and stakeholder engagement, while strengthening my skills in collaboration, creativity, and taking initiative. It also gave me a better understanding of the intersection between gender-related issues and mental health.
