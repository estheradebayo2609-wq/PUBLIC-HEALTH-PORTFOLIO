Project 3: NYSC Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) CDS

Organization: National Youth Service Corps (NYSC)

Role: Vice President, Sustainable Development Goals CDS

Project: SDG 3 – Good Health and Well-being in the Workplace

Project Overview

As Vice President of the NYSC SDGs CDS, I supported an outreach at Access Bank, Akute, Ogun State; focused on promoting health and well-being in the workplace.

Goal

To promote SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being) by raising awareness among bank staff about workplace safety, mental health, ergonomics, and the importance of creating a healthy, safe, and supportive work environment.

My Contribution

Supported the planning and coordination of the outreach and contributed to engaging bank staff on workplace health and safety, HSE practices, ergonomics, mental health, healthy communication, and building supportive work environments.

Impact & Outcome

The outreach provided staff with practical awareness on promoting safer, healthier, and more supportive workplaces. A focus group discussion was also held with five staff members representing different departments, including Security, Customer Care, Marketing, and Sanitation, allowing discussions to be tailored to the specific context and health and safety needs of their roles.

Skills Demonstrated

Leadership • Project Coordination • Public Speaking • Facilitation • Communication • Health Advocacy • Stakeholder Engagement • Teamwork

Lessons Learned

Being part of one of the many outreaches organized through the SDGs CDS introduced me to how project management and public health can be applied across different settings. It strengthened my understanding of planning, coordination, advocacy, and stakeholder engagement in promoting health and well-being.
